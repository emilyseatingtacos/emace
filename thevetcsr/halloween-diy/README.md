# A Little Boo at the Front Desk

24-page original TheVetCSR Halloween craft manual. Run `python3 build.py` with reportlab installed. Output is written to `../../output/pdf` relative to the script's parent directory; see the OUT constant for the exact path.

## Intelligence and architecture
Audience: busy veterinary reception teams. Goals: printable, reusable projects with clear assembly instructions and minimal disruption. Six modules: ghost-pet display; black-cat welcome sign; display-only pumpkin cone; useful desk cards; seated doodle hunt; team appreciation notes. Each module separates instructions from reusable templates. Default designs use flat mounting and seated participation.

## QA and rerouting
24 pages rendered and reviewed as a contact sheet. Automated text bounds check passed. Cone template heading clearance repaired. Cone circumference corrected: two quarter-annuli with inner radius 120 pt have total inner arc 120*pi pt = 5.236 in before overlap; approximately 4.7 in after 0.5 in overlap. Calibration squares are 72 x 72 pt. Final cone page inspected separately after repair.

Physical print/cut/assembly testing has NOT been performed. Completion times are estimates. Test the cone and printer scaling before describing the product as tested or listing it as ready for sale. The manual includes stop/remove/reroute/recheck instructions for clinic use.

No private user contact data or paid source-product files are included. This draft is not merged or automatically published to Etsy.
